Questions

Do I need to install anything?

No. SignBridge runs in your browser. Open the page and start typing or speaking immediately.

What happens to text I type or speech I speak?

Everything is processed locally. No text, audio, or video ever leaves your device.

What sign languages are supported?

The avatar currently signs American Sign Language (ASL). Unknown words are automatically fingerspelled letter-by-letter using the ASL manual alphabet (A–Z).

Can I use my voice?

Yes — tap the microphone button. Your browser converts speech to text locally, then the avatar signs it immediately.

How accurate are the signs?

Signs are based on published ASL resources. Always have a Deaf signer review before using in medical, legal, or safety-critical contexts.
